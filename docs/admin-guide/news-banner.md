# News banner

**Where:** Admin > Content > News, in the editor of a news item or announcement.

Tick **Show in the dashboard banner** and fill in: the banner text (up to 140 characters; the title
if left empty), an optional link (a page in the app such as `/jobs`, or a full https address), the
type (Announcement, News or Urgent), and when it starts and ends (Lagos time; it must end after it
starts and in the future). A live preview shows how it will look.

Members see active banners as one slow line at the top of their dashboard. Urgent ones come first,
then the newest. It pauses when the pointer or keyboard focus is on it, has a Pause button, and the
X closes it for that member for good. For people who ask their device for less motion it does not
move: one item shows at a time with Previous and Next. Screen readers get an ordinary list.

The News list says whether each banner is Scheduled, Live or Ended, and **End banner now** stops
one early without taking the story down. Expired banners disappear on their own.
