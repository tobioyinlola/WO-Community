# Posts and feed

**Where:** Admin > Content > Posts and feed. **New post** opens the composer with the team's options:

- **Post as the WO Community team** (shown with a "WO Community team" badge and no admin name) or as
  yourself.
- **Pin to the top**, with an optional date when the pin lapses on its own.
- **Allow comments** on or off.
- **Who can see it:** all members, mentors only, or members only.
- **Publish later:** pick a time; the post stays out of the feed until then and appears in the
  Scheduled view, where it can be edited, moved or cancelled.

Team posts are never held for review and do not count against the daily allowance. You can also
edit, pin, feature, hide or remove any member's post or comment (a reason is needed to hide or
remove). The author is notified, and each action is audited.
