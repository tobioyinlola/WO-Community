# Approval alerts

When something needs an admin's approval, the admins who can approve it are told in the app and by
email: a new member request (after they confirm their email), a job waiting for review, a mentor
application, a course access request, reported content, and a new support request.

- The **first** item of a kind is emailed at once. More items of the same kind within 15 minutes go
  in **one summary** afterwards, so nobody is flooded.
- Each email names what happened and who did it, and has one button, **Review request**, that opens
  the right page (a member opens in the member drawer).
- Once a day, anyone with something still waiting after 24 hours gets a short summary.
- Each admin can switch each kind on or off in Settings > Notifications. Everything stays on by
  default, and you can only see the kinds you are allowed to approve.
- An item is recorded once however often it is raised, so a retry never sends a second email.
