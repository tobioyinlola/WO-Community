# Feature card photos

The six cards under "Everything a founder needs" on the home page each show a photo from this
folder. Until a file is here, the card shows its forest gradient and icon instead, so nothing breaks.

Use photos you own or are licensed to use (Unsplash and Pexels photos are free for commercial use).
Landscape, 640 x 400 px or larger at 16:10, WebP, under 80 KB each.

| File | Card | What it should show |
|------|------|---------------------|
| `directory.webp` | Find your people | Founders talking together at a community meetup |
| `mentors.webp` | Learn from people who did it | A mentor and a founder in a one to one conversation |
| `launch-toolkit.webp` | Launch with a plan | A founder working on a business plan at a laptop |
| `courses.webp` | Keep learning | Founders learning together in a workshop |
| `jobs.webp` | Hire and get hired | A startup team at work |
| `events.webp` | Meet in person | A founder pitching on stage at a demo day |

If a photo shows something different, change its `photoAlt` text in
`frontend/src/pages/public/Landing.tsx` to describe it.
