## 2.1 About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how WithOnyeka ("WO Community", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, shares and protects personal data when you use the WO Community website, web application and related services (the "Platform"). We are the data controller for the personal data described here. We handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 ("NDPA") and other data protection laws that apply to us.

Please read this policy together with our Terms and Conditions. If you have questions, see section 2.14.

## 2.2 Personal data we collect

**Information you give us**

- **Account and application details:** name, email address, password (stored in a protected form), phone number (if you add it), how you heard about us, and who referred or invited you.
- **Profile information:** photo, headline, role, company, industry, stage, location, bio, links, interests and goals, and the preferences you set.
- **Content you post:** posts, comments, event registrations, job listings, mentor profile and availability, and your messages to us.
- **Support requests:** the subject, message, category and any attachments you send us through the in-app form, and our replies.
- **Learning information:** courses you enrol in, lessons you complete, progress and certificates issued to you.
- **Mentorship information:** your availability, pricing (free or paid), session requests and bookings.
- **Payments:** if and when we switch on payments, we collect the details needed to process them through a payment provider. We do not store full card details ourselves.

**Information we collect automatically**

- **Device and usage data:** IP address, browser and device type, pages and features used, dates and times, referring pages and error logs.
- **Cookies and similar technologies:** see section 2.9.
- **Security and audit records:** sign-in activity and a record of important actions on accounts and in the admin area, kept to protect members and the Platform.

**Information from others**

- A member or administrator who invites or refers you may give us your email address and name.
- Videos embedded from YouTube may collect data directly under YouTube's own policies when you play them.

**Sensitive personal data.** We do not ask for sensitive personal data (such as health, religion, ethnicity, political opinion or biometric data). Please do not include it in your profile or posts. If you choose to share it, you agree to our handling it as part of Your Content.

## 2.3 How we use personal data and our legal bases

| What we do | Why (legal basis) |
|---|---|
| Review your application, create and run your account, verify your email, keep you signed in | To perform our agreement with you |
| Show your profile in the member directory and let members find and contact each other | To perform our agreement with you, and our legitimate interest in running the community |
| Provide courses, track progress, issue certificates, run events, mentorship and jobs | To perform our agreement with you |
| Send you service emails (verification, approval, password reset, security, support replies, reminders) | To perform our agreement with you and our legitimate interest in keeping accounts secure |
| Send you community news, digests and announcements | Your consent, or our legitimate interest, with an unsubscribe link in every message |
| Respond to your support requests and enquiries | To perform our agreement and our legitimate interests |
| Keep the Platform safe, prevent fraud and abuse, enforce our Terms and Code of Conduct, keep audit logs | Our legitimate interests and, where needed, legal obligation |
| Improve the Platform and understand how it is used | Our legitimate interests |
| Meet legal, tax and regulatory duties and respond to lawful requests | Legal obligation |
| Process payments in future | To perform our agreement with you |

Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time (see section 2.10). We do not use your personal data to make decisions about you by solely automated means that have a legal or similarly significant effect.

## 2.4 What other members can see

- Other approved members can see the profile information you choose to add, and your public posts and comments. Your email address and phone number are not shown to other members unless you choose to display them.
- Your job listings, mentor profile, event registrations (where an event shows attendees) and course certificates may be visible as described on the relevant page.
- Anything you post in a community space may be seen, copied or shared by other members, even if you later delete it. Please think before you share.

## 2.5 Who we share personal data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only as follows:

- **Service providers (processors)** that help us run the Platform, under written agreements and only on our instructions. These include providers of: hosting and databases, file storage, email delivery, video hosting and embedding (YouTube), analytics and error monitoring, and, in future, payment processing. The providers we currently use include Railway, Vercel, Cloudflare, Resend and Google (YouTube).
- **Mentors, employers and event organisers**, only where you choose to contact or book them, apply to them, or register for their event.
- **The person who referred you**, who may be told that you joined or applied.
- **Professional advisers**, such as lawyers, accountants and insurers, where needed.
- **Authorities and regulators**, courts or other parties, where required by law or to protect rights, safety and security.
- **A buyer or successor**, if our business is reorganised, merged or sold, in which case the new owner must honour this policy.

## 2.6 Job applications

Applications for jobs listed on the Platform are made directly to the employer by email or through the employer's own link. We do not receive, store or process application materials. Employers are responsible for their own handling of those details.

## 2.7 Transfers outside Nigeria

Our providers may store or process personal data in other countries, including the United States and other places outside Nigeria. Where we transfer personal data abroad, we do so in line with the NDPA, for example by transferring to countries or providers that offer an adequate level of protection, or by using contractual and other safeguards.

## 2.8 How long we keep personal data

We keep personal data only for as long as we need it for the purposes in this policy:

- **Account and profile data:** while your account is open, and for up to 12 months after it closes unless we need to keep it longer for a legal reason.
- **Applications that are not approved:** up to 12 months.
- **Support requests:** up to 24 months after they are resolved.
- **Audit logs and security records:** at least 24 months.
- **Learning records and certificates:** while your account is open, so that certificates can be verified, and afterwards as long as needed to verify them.
- **Marketing preferences and suppression records** (for example people who unsubscribed or whose email bounced): as long as needed to respect your choices.
- **Backups:** deleted on a regular cycle.
- **Tax and financial records, if any:** for the period required by law.

When we no longer need personal data, we delete it or make it anonymous.

## 2.9 Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your settings (such as light or dark theme), keep the Platform secure, and understand how it is used.

- **Essential cookies** are needed to run the Platform and cannot be switched off in our systems.
- **Preference cookies** remember your choices.
- **Analytics cookies**, if we use them, tell us which pages are used. We will ask for your consent where the law requires.
- **Third party content**, such as embedded YouTube videos, may set its own cookies when you play it.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may stop parts of the Platform from working.

## 2.10 Your rights

Under the NDPA and other laws that apply, you may have the right to:

- be told how your data is used and **access** a copy of it;
- have inaccurate data **corrected** or completed;
- have your data **deleted**, in certain circumstances;
- **restrict** or **object** to some uses, including direct marketing;
- **withdraw your consent** at any time, without affecting earlier use;
- receive your data in a structured, commonly used format (**data portability**);
- not be subject to a decision based only on automated processing that has a significant effect on you;
- **complain** to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) if you think we have not handled your data lawfully.

To use your rights, send a request through Contact support in the app and choose "Privacy request". We may need to confirm your identity first. We aim to respond within 30 days. You can edit most profile information yourself in your settings, and unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in each email or in your notification settings.

## 2.11 Email communications

We send service emails because they are part of running your account. We send community news, digests and announcements only to members who have verified their email address, and each one has an unsubscribe link. If you unsubscribe from non-essential emails, we will still send messages that are necessary for your account, such as password resets and security alerts. We use an email provider to deliver messages and may record delivery, open and click events and bounce and complaint information, to keep our sending healthy and our lists accurate.

## 2.12 Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, access controls based on role, audit logging, secure hosting and regular backups. No system is perfectly secure. You help by using a strong, unique password and keeping it private. If a personal data breach occurs that is likely to put people at risk, we will notify the NDPC and, where required, affected people, within the time the law sets.

## 2.13 Children

The Platform is for adults. You must be 18 or over to join, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If we learn we have done so, we will delete it. If you believe a child has given us personal data, please tell us.

## 2.14 Changes and contact

We may update this policy. If we make an important change, we will tell you by email or in the Platform before it takes effect, and we will update the "last updated" date.

**Contact:** WithOnyeka, operating as WO Community. For privacy questions or requests, use Contact support in the app and choose "Privacy request".
