## 3.1 Why this exists

WO Community exists so that founders, operators and investors can meet the right people, learn from each other and build. That only works when people feel safe, respected and free to be honest. This Code of Conduct explains how we expect everyone to behave, what is not acceptable, and what happens when someone gets it wrong. It applies to every member, mentor, administrator and guest, and everywhere the community meets: the Platform, events (online and in person), mentorship sessions, and any space we run for the community.

## 3.2 Our principles

1. **Be respectful.** Treat every member with courtesy and good faith. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
2. **Be generous.** Share what you know, make introductions, and give feedback kindly.
3. **Be honest.** Be truthful about who you are, your business, your results and your intentions.
4. **Be professional.** Remember that people here are building real businesses and careers. Keep it constructive.
5. **Be inclusive.** Welcome people of every background, gender, tribe, nationality, religion, age, ability and level of experience.
6. **Protect trust.** What members share in the community is for the community.

## 3.3 What we expect

- Use your real name and accurate profile information.
- Keep posts useful, relevant and respectful. Give context and credit sources.
- Ask before you promote. Share your own work where the space allows it, but give more than you take.
- Respect other people's time and replies. Do not pressure anyone for a response, introduction, investment or sale.
- Keep sensitive discussions private. Do not share screenshots, private messages, personal details or what someone told you in confidence outside the community without their permission.
- Give credit, and respect other people's intellectual property.
- Speak up. If you see something that breaks this Code, report it.

## 3.4 What is not acceptable

**Harassment and abuse.** Insults, bullying, intimidation, stalking, threats, repeated unwanted contact or messages, or encouraging others to do any of these.

**Discrimination and hate.** Content or behaviour that demeans or attacks people because of their race, ethnicity, tribe, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, age, disability, marital status or social background.

**Sexual misconduct.** Unwelcome sexual comments, advances or images, or using the community to look for dates or sexual contact. Sexually explicit material is not allowed anywhere on the Platform.

**Violence and illegal activity.** Threats or glorification of violence, and anything that breaks the law or encourages others to.

**Dishonesty and fraud.** Lying about your identity, credentials, track record, company or funding; fake testimonials or reviews; impersonation; fake jobs, fake investment opportunities or fake mentor offers; scams of any kind.

**Spam and unsolicited promotion.** Mass or repeated posts, cold direct messages to many members, advertising that is off topic, and affiliate or referral link dropping.

**Financial schemes.** Pyramid or Ponzi schemes, multi-level marketing recruitment, get-rich-quick or "guaranteed return" offers, and unlicensed offers of securities, crypto schemes or financial products. If you raise capital through the community, you are responsible for following the law, and you must not promise returns or press anyone to invest. Nothing here is investment advice.

**Misuse of member data.** Scraping or exporting the directory, building lead lists, selling or sharing member details, or using contact details for anything except genuine community purposes.

**Privacy violations.** Posting other people's private information (such as phone numbers, addresses, financial details or private conversations) without permission.

**Jobs misuse.** Fake or misleading job posts, listings that discriminate, charging applicants any fee, or collecting applicants' personal data you do not need.

**Misconduct in mentorship.** Mentors must not use their position to exploit, pressure, solicit money or romantic involvement from, or mislead a mentee. Mentees must respect the mentor's time and boundaries. Both must keep what is shared in a session confidential unless there is permission to share it. If a paid arrangement is agreed, both sides must honour it fairly.

**Platform misuse.** Trying to break, overload or bypass the security of the Platform; sharing your account; creating fake or duplicate accounts; or evading a suspension.

**Retaliation.** Punishing, harassing or threatening someone for reporting a concern or taking part in an investigation.

## 3.5 Conduct at events and in sessions

The same Code applies at events (online and in person) and in mentorship sessions. Organisers and administrators may remove anyone from an event who breaks this Code, without a refund if there was a fee. Do not record, photograph or share what happens in private sessions without the consent of everyone involved. At in-person events, respect the venue and look out for each other.

## 3.6 Conflicts of interest

If you are a mentor, administrator or organiser and you have a personal, financial or business interest in a decision or recommendation (for example, you are recommending a company you own or invest in), say so openly. Administrators must not make decisions about their own conduct or accounts.

## 3.7 How to report a concern

- **In the app:** use Report on a post, comment or profile, or send a request through Contact support and choose "Report a problem". Include links or screenshots if you can.
- **If someone is in immediate danger,** contact local emergency services first.
- **Confidentiality.** We keep reports confidential and share details only with the people who need them to look into the matter, or when the law requires.
- **No retaliation.** We will not tolerate anyone being punished for reporting in good faith.

## 3.8 What we do about reports

We review each report fairly and promptly, and we look at the facts and context before deciding. Depending on how serious the matter is and whether it has happened before, we may:

1. **Remove content** and explain what the issue was.
2. **Give an informal reminder** or a private conversation.
3. **Issue a formal warning**, recorded on the account.
4. **Restrict features**, for example limiting posting, messaging, mentoring or posting jobs for a period.
5. **Suspend the account** for a period.
6. **Remove the member permanently**, and where necessary report the matter to the police or other authorities.

We may skip steps and act immediately where there is a serious risk to people or the community. Where we can, we will tell the member what they did wrong and what decision was made, though we may not share details of a report or who made it.

## 3.9 Appeals

If you think a decision about you was wrong, you can appeal within 14 days by sending a request through Contact support and choosing "Appeal a decision", explaining why. A different administrator, where possible, will review the decision and reply. Their decision is final within the community, without affecting your legal rights.

## 3.10 Changes

We may update this Code as the community grows. We will tell members about important changes, and the "last updated" date will show when it last changed. By continuing to use the Platform you agree to follow the current version.

*Thank you for helping us build a community people are proud to belong to.*
